[Arival](https://arival.travel/article/bookings-quiet-return-to-tours-attractions/)

Spunto: valutare pricing per fascia oraria/data sui nostri tour piu richiesti a Roma per recuperare margine, non solo volume. [Arival](https://arival.travel/article/is-your-pricing-stuck-in-the-past) (riferito anche da [Travolution](https://www.travolution.com/en/experiences/seven-in-10-tour-and-attraction-operators-still-use-fixed-pricing-arival-study-finds))
