# Digest Generale - 08/11/2026

[PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/news/technology/tour-operators-question-ota-rankings-ai-changes-travel-discovery)

Valutare con attenzione eventuali proposte analoghe sui nostri listing prima di accettare aumenti di fee. [PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/news/technology/tour-operators-question-ota-rankings-ai-changes-travel-discovery)

Spunto: rafforzare i canali di prenotazione diretta (sito, WhatsApp, partnership hotel) per ridurre dipendenza dalle OTA. [Skift](https://skift.com/2026/11/02/skift-commerce-summit-five-decisions-in-the-room)

[Arival](https://arival.travel/research/travel-experiences-2026-market-highlights-report/)

[Skift](https://skift.com/2026/11/02/skift-commerce-summit-five-decisions-in-the-room)
